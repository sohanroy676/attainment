## What [College name] keeps about you in Attainment
- Your name and your roll number or other student identifiers (university registration number, hall ticket number).
- The batch, programme, section and courses you were enrolled in.
- Your marks in internal assessments, laboratory work and semester examinations, and any change made to a mark (who, when, why).

Results are worked out for a section or a course as a whole. Attainment does not keep a per-student score of its own.

## Why
To work out and report how well each course outcome and programme outcome is attained, as the college's accreditation and quality processes require, and to keep the signed reports reproducible.

## Who can see it
Your lecturers (their own sections), the course coordinator, the head of department, the IQAC team, the examination branch (semester marks), the principal and internal or external auditors, each only for the records their role covers. Records of other colleges are never visible.

## How long
Your name and identifiers are kept for [5] years after your batch ends. You are told [30] days before they are replaced with anonymous labels. After that, your marks stay in the college's signed results without your name.

## Your rights
You can ask [contact: IQAC office, email] to
- see everything held about you (a file is produced from the system),
- correct a wrong name or roll number,
- ask about how your data is used.
